Privacy Policy

Checkup Lite, published by MoleCare LTD. Last updated 9 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Checkup Lite has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting of our own, and no servers. It does not collect, store or transmit personal data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your Mac

  • Folder sizes and a free-space history, kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Checkup.
  • A log of removals you confirmed, kept in ~/Library/Logs/Checkup.
  • The folder you granted, remembered as a macOS security-scoped bookmark.

You can delete these files at any time; the app recreates what it needs.

The optional assistant

If, and only if, you connect an AI provider in the Assistant section, the app sends that provider a short text summary of your scan (folder paths under your home folder, sizes, and finding titles) together with your question. It never sends file contents. The provider you choose may be a program on your own Mac, a machine on your own network, or a cloud service you have an account with; their handling of that text is governed by their terms, not ours. API keys you enter are stored only in your macOS Keychain and never leave your Mac except in requests to that provider.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
